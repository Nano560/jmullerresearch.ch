About Me

Education

Electrical engineer by formation, physicist at heart, and philosopher by night.

I completed my PhD at the Centre for Immersive Wave Experimentation at ETH Zurich, working on Acoustic Immersive Wave Experimentation.

You can find my thesis Advancing 3D Acoustic Immersive Wave Experimentation in the ETH Research Collection.

My publications are listed on Google Scholar.
