Theoretical and numerical work

  1. 2020–2021

    Topological classification of SU(1,1) scattering systems MSc thesis, EPFL

    Investigated the topological classification of a one-dimensional multiple-scattering system described by the lossless, reciprocal SU(1,1) transfer-matrix group; illustrated the classification with a worked example and outlined its generalisation to broader symmetry groups.

  2. 2021–2025

    Kirchhoff integral for wavefield extrapolation PhD, ETH Zürich

    Evaluated the time-domain Kirchhoff integral analytically for planar and spherical surfaces, checked the results numerically, and tested the extrapolation on a 3D measurement in water (hydrophone scans over two concentric spheres).

  3. 2025–2026

    Acoustic disguising PhD and independent research

    Showed that cloaking and holography are two limits of one boundary operation with immersive boundary conditions, and demonstrated cloaking, holography and disguising (a sphere that scatters like a cube) in 3D finite-difference simulations. See Acoustic Disguising.

Research experience

  1. 2021–2025

    Doctoral researcher ETH Zürich, CIWE

    Worked on immersive boundary conditions, which couple a physical experiment to a numerical simulation in real time, so that a finite laboratory volume behaves as though embedded in a larger numerical domain.

    • Demonstrated acoustic cloning experimentally in a 2D air waveguide (first-author paper in Physical Review Applied, selected as an APS Physics Focus article): retrieved scattering Green's functions from measured data by wavefield separation and multidimensional deconvolution, then reproduced three scatterers, and modified versions of them, in real time.
    • Brought the 3D WaveLab (176 hydrophones and 121 piezoelectric sources in water) into operation: acquisition and control software (Python, xarray), in-place calibration of channel gains and delays, and automated measurement scans.
    • Implemented finite-difference time-domain codes for acoustic wave propagation (Julia, on GPU) and used them for 3D simulations of cloaking, holography and disguising.
  2. 2025–present

    Independent researcher

    • Working on the theory and numerical examples from the PhD thesis towards journal publications.
    • First-author manuscript on acoustic disguising (cloaking and holography with immersive boundary conditions), submitted to Physical Review Research; preprint on arXiv, code archived on Zenodo.
  3. Jun–Sep 2019

    Research intern, Radio Frequency Group CERN

    RF Group under Dr. Rama Calaga. Characterisation of accelerator RF cavities:

    • Cross-validated laboratory measurements against CST (RF / Particle Studio) electromagnetic simulations and existing literature.
    • Performed wire and bead-pull measurements on an accelerator cavity; measured and fitted the response of feedback-coupled RF cavities.
    • Extracted key RF figures of merit from the data (MATLAB / Python) and presented results internally.
  4. Feb–Jun 2019

    Master's semester project, implantable antennas EPFL, MAG group

    Under Prof. Anja Skrivervik. Simulated implantable antenna concepts (ANSYS HFSS / CST) to characterise electromagnetic propagation through lossy, inhomogeneous layered tissue and verify transmitter–receiver link characteristics.
