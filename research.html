Acoustic Disguising

2026 · submitted to Physical Review Research

Cloaking and holography as one operation: a closed surface that records the incoming sound and emits a prepared response can hide an object, imitate another, or make a sphere scatter like a cube. Shown in 3D simulations.

Acoustic Cloning

2023 · Physical Review Applied

Reproducing physical objects as acoustic holograms in real time, demonstrated in a laboratory experiment in a 2D air waveguide.
